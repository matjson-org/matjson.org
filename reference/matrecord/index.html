Overview

MatRecordJSON is planned as the common evidence layer between source documents and automated checking. It will normalize material identity, reported results, heat treatment, tests, NDE, certification, and extraction provenance without depending on one mill certificate layout.

Evidence-centric

The record reports what the source document says; it does not independently decide compliance.

Layout-independent

Different supplier formats normalize into one consistent evidence structure.
