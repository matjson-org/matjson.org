Fields

FieldTypeStatusMeaning
idstringRequiredStable normalized identifier for the grade, class, type, condition, or process variant.
designationobjectRequiredSource designation details, such as grade, class, type, UNS number, condition, or process.
aliasesarrayOptionalAlternative designations by which the same normalized grade entry may be identified.
chemistryOne Of (2)RequiredHeat- and/or product-analysis acceptance requirements for the grade, or an explicit extraction-gap marker.
mechanicalOne Of (3)OptionalTensile, yield, elongation, reduction-of-area, or related mechanical acceptance requirements.
hardnessOne Of (3)OptionalHardness limits, including formally represented alternatives where the source permits different scales.
impactOne Of (3)OptionalImpact energy, test temperature, specimen, orientation, and applicability requirements.
heat_treatmentOne Of (3)OptionalRequired thermal condition, temperature, cooling route, applicability, and source.
