Overview

MatCheckJSON is planned to preserve the exact requirement evaluated, its origin, applicability, evidence, comparison, result, explanation, and review state. It will distinguish failure from missing evidence, missing requisition input, non-applicability, and manual-review conditions.

Explainable outcomes

Every result retains the requirement, evidence, comparison, and reason.

More than pass/fail

Missing evidence, requisition gaps, not-applicable rules, and manual review are distinct outcomes.
