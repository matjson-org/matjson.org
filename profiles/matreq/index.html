MatReqJSON

Machine-readable application, service, fabrication, equipment-code, and purchaser material requirements.

Working draft · v0.2.matreq.json
MatReqJSONOverviewView schemaDownload schemaExamples

Overview

MatReqJSON represents atomic rules imposed in addition to a base material specification and activated through a material requisition or contract document.

Rule anatomy

  • Stable rule identifier and category
  • Source normativity and project activation
  • Material, product-form, component, weld-region, service, and fabrication targets
  • Boolean applicability conditions
  • Typed requirement behavior and effect on MatSpec
  • Verification evidence and source provenance

Requisition activation

Whole-document, scoped section, annex, clause, and direct purchaser invocations are supported conceptually. The material requisition remains the project-specific source of truth.

Known work

  • Executable decision graphs
  • Multidimensional tables and curves
  • Effective-requirement resolver
  • Formal formulas, sampling, and retest models
