MatSpecJSON — JSON schema for material specifications

Machine-readable intrinsic acceptance criteria for a material or product specification.

Working draft · v0.2.10.matspec.json
MatSpecJSONOverviewReference docsRaw schemaDownload schemaExamples

Overview

MatSpecJSON represents what a product must satisfy to legitimately conform to its base material specification, including imported general requirements and adoption overlays.

Open the linked schema

Read the actual JSON Schema with stable object anchors, linked $ref values, and human-readable definition guides.

Open linked schema

Primary content

  • Grades, classes, types, UNS numbers, and aliases
  • Chemistry and composite chemistry constraints
  • Mechanical, hardness, impact, and heat-treatment requirements
  • Test methods, sampling, frequencies, retests, certification, and marking
  • Explicit distinction between “no requirement” and “not extracted”

Design principle

MatSpec is grade-centric because product specifications generally organize requirements around named grades or conditions.

Known work

  • Extract common primitives into MatJSON Core
  • Formalize formulas and statistical acceptance
  • Strengthen imported general-requirement and adoption-overlay precedence
  • Add stricter completeness profiles
